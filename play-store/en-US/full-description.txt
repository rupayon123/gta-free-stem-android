Find real, free STEM opportunities across the Greater Toronto Area.

GTA FREE STEM brings programs, workshops, library events, camps, volunteer-hour opportunities, mentorship, co-op and SHSM pathways into one easy place to explore. Every listing in the app is intended to be free for participants.

Explore with confidence

• Search and filter current opportunities by city, region, age, language, pathway and community focus.
• Browse a compact list or a zero-cost schematic map—no paid map service or live map tiles required.
• See New Finds and sort nearby opportunities by distance after choosing to share one approximate, foreground-only location fix.
• Open full details, provider registration pages, original sources and directions.

Keep your hunt organized

• Save opportunities on your device and revisit current or archived listings.
• Turn on optional local alerts for new matches. Android checks the public feed periodically and compares matches on your device; there is no push-notification account or subscription.
• Open Opportunities and High School pathways quickly through app shortcuts and supported GTA FREE STEM links.

Made for the GTA community

• Switch among 18 app languages, including right-to-left layouts.
• Use light, dark or system theme.
• Navigate with adaptive phone and tablet layouts, large-text support, screen-reader labels and Android-standard controls.

Private by design

No sign-in is required. GTA FREE STEM contains no ads or analytics SDKs. Your optional profile, saved opportunities, searches, filters and alert history stay on your device. Nearby uses approximate location only when you ask, keeps the fix in memory, and does not send or save it.

Opportunity details can change. Always confirm dates, eligibility, accessibility and availability with the provider before registering. External provider websites have their own terms and privacy practices.
